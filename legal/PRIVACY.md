# Orgodesk Privacy Policy

*Draft for [SELLER LEGAL NAME] to review. Last updated [DATE].*

**What stays on your PC.** Orgodesk keeps your layout (Orgos, presets, pages), settings, license record and an error log
in `%APPDATA%\orgodesk` on your computer. None of it is sent anywhere.

**What is sent, and why.** To check your license, Orgodesk sends your **license key** and Orgodesk's **product id** to
Gumroad's license service (`api.gumroad.com`) when you activate a key and about once a week afterwards. Gumroad replies
whether the key is valid, how many times it has been activated, and whether the purchase was refunded or disputed.
Gumroad also sees the network address your request comes from, as any web service does. Orgodesk sends nothing else:
no file names, no usage statistics, no crash reports, no advertising identifiers.

**Your purchase.** When you buy, Gumroad processes your payment and email address under
[Gumroad's privacy policy](https://gumroad.com/privacy). We receive your email address and purchase details from
Gumroad, and use them only to provide the license, support and refunds. [SELLER: say here if you will also send
product updates by email, and how to opt out.]

**Support email.** If you email us, we use your message and address only to answer it.

**Your choices.** You can delete everything Orgodesk stores by uninstalling it (Settings > Apps) and choosing to delete
your data, or by deleting `%APPDATA%\orgodesk`. To ask what we hold about you or to have it deleted, write to
[SUPPORT EMAIL].

**Changes.** If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on the Orgodesk product page with a new date.
