# Orgodesk Privacy Policy

*Draft, not yet reviewed by a lawyer. Effective date: [DATE].*

This policy covers the Orgodesk desktop program and its sale. The seller and data controller is
[SELLER LEGAL NAME], [STATE], USA. Contact: [SUPPORT EMAIL].

## The short version

Orgodesk does not track you. It has no analytics, no advertising, no crash upload and no account. The only thing it
sends over the internet is your license key, to check that the key is valid.

## What stays on your PC

Orgodesk stores these on your computer only, and never sends them anywhere:

- Your layout (Orgos, presets, pages), settings, license record and an error log, in `%APPDATA%\orgodesk`.
- A scrambled fingerprint (a SHA-256 hash) of your license key, in the Windows registry under
  `HKCU\Software\Orgodesk`, so that reinstalling on the same PC does not use up another activation.

## What is sent, to whom, and why

| Data | Sent to | When | Why (legal basis under GDPR) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Your license key and Orgodesk's product id | Gumroad, Inc. (USA), the store that issued the key | When you activate a key, and about once a week after | To provide the licensed software you bought (performance of a contract) and to prevent misuse of keys (legitimate interest) |

Gumroad answers whether the key is valid, how many times it has been activated, and whether the purchase was refunded
or disputed. As with any internet request, Gumroad's servers also see the network (IP) address the request comes from.
Orgodesk sends no file names, no desktop contents, no usage statistics and no device identifiers.

A key whose purchase was refunded, charged back or disputed stops working automatically. No other automated decisions
are made about you.

## Your purchase

Purchases are handled by Gumroad as the seller of record: Gumroad collects your payment details and email address
under [Gumroad's privacy policy](https://gumroad.com/privacy). We never see your card details. From Gumroad we
receive your email address, what you bought and when, and your license key. We use this only to provide your license,
answer support requests, handle refunds and keep the sales records the law requires. [SELLER: if you will email buyers
about updates, say so here and that they can unsubscribe.]

## Support email

If you write to us, we use your message and email address only to answer you. If you send a log file, it may contain
the names and paths of items on your desktop; send it only if you are comfortable with that.

## Who we share data with

Only the service providers needed to sell and support Orgodesk: Gumroad (store, payments, license service) and our
email provider. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

## Where data is processed

We and Gumroad are in the United States, so your data is processed there. If you are in the European Economic Area,
the United Kingdom or Switzerland, that is a transfer outside your country; it is necessary to provide what you bought.

## How long we keep it

Purchase records: for as long as tax and accounting law requires. Support emails: up to [2] years after the last
message, then deleted. Data on your PC stays until you delete it.

## Your choices and rights

- **On your PC:** uninstall Orgodesk from Settings > Apps and choose to delete your data, or delete
  `%APPDATA%\orgodesk` and the `HKCU\Software\Orgodesk` registry key yourself.
- **What we hold:** you can ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, or to correct or delete it, or
  object to how we use it, by writing to [SUPPORT EMAIL]. We answer within 30 days. We may need to keep purchase
  records the law requires.
- **In the EEA or UK** you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.
- **California residents:** we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in California law.

Giving a license key is required to use Orgodesk after the trial; nothing else is required.

## Do Not Track

Orgodesk does not track you across websites or apps, so there is nothing for a Do Not Track signal to switch off. The
store page is run by Gumroad; its handling of such signals is described in Gumroad's own policy.

## Children

Orgodesk is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the new version will be posted on the Orgodesk product page with a new
effective date before it takes effect.
