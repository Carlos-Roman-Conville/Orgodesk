## How to ask

Reply to your Gumroad receipt email, or write to [SUPPORT EMAIL] with the email address you bought with. Refunds go
back to your original payment method through Gumroad, usually within a few business days.

## If Windows won't let it run

Orgodesk is from a new, independent developer and is not yet code-signed. On most PCs Windows shows a warning you can
click past (More info, then Run anyway). On PCs with **Smart App Control** turned on, Windows blocks unsigned apps and
offers no way past the block. If that is your PC and you don't want to turn Smart App Control off, ask for a refund:
you will get one, at any time.

## After a refund

The license key from a refunded purchase stops working. Your Orgos and layout stay on your PC.

## Please contact us before disputing a charge

If something went wrong with your purchase, write to us first; we will fix it or refund you. A chargeback takes longer
for you and also disables the key.

## Your legal rights

This policy is in addition to any rights you have under the consumer law where you live, such as the 14-day right of
withdrawal for online purchases in the EU and UK. It does not limit them.
